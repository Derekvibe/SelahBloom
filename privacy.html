Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the SelahBloom mobile application (“Application”) for mobile devices. The Application is developed by [Publisher legal name to be added] (“Service Provider,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). The Application is designed to provide daily affirmations, scripture, and personal reflection on your device.

By downloading, installing, or using the Application, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with these practices, please discontinue use of the Application.

1. Information We Collect

The Application does not require an account and does not send your journal entries, selected moods, saved affirmations, saved scripture, or reminder preferences to a SelahBloom server. These items are stored locally on your device to provide the app’s features.

The Application does not collect precise location, contact details, or account credentials. It does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, and it does not automatically send us your IP address, in-app activity, or device operating system.

If you choose to share an affirmation or scripture, the selected text is passed to your device’s sharing interface. You choose whether to share it and which recipient or service to use.

This website is a set of static pages and does not use cookies, advertising, or analytics scripts. The website’s hosting provider may process standard request information, such as IP address, request time, and browser details, to deliver and protect the site. The host’s privacy policy describes its practices.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information stored by the Application on your device is used to display your reflections and saved items, remember your reminder preference, and provide the features you choose to use. We do not receive that locally stored app information.

When you enable a daily reminder, the Application asks your operating system for notification permission and schedules the reminder locally on your device. No account or remote notification server is required for this reminder.

3. Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share your locally stored app content with third parties because it is not sent to us. If you choose to share content, the device’s sharing feature and the recipient you select handle that content under their own privacy terms.

The website’s hosting provider may process technical request information as described above. We do not operate an advertising or analytics integration on the website.

4. Use of Artificial Intelligence

The Application does not use an AI service to process your reflections or personalize content. Your entries are not sent to an AI provider.

5. Your Rights and Opt-Out Options

You can delete individual reflections and remove saved items using the controls in the Application. You can stop future local reminders by turning them off in the Application or changing notification permissions in your device settings.

You can stop using the Application and remove its locally stored data through your device’s app and storage settings. The effect of uninstalling an app may vary by device, operating system, and backup settings.

6. Data Retention

Reflections, moods, favorites, and reminder preferences remain in local app storage until you delete them or remove the app data from your device. We do not retain this app content on a SelahBloom server. Your device’s backup settings may affect whether app data is included in a device backup.

7. Children’s Privacy

The Application does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It does not require an account, and the reflection and favorite features store their content locally on the device. Parents and guardians can manage access to the Application and device data using the controls available on the device.

8. Security

Your app content is stored in the Application’s local storage on your device. Protecting access to the device and its backups is important; the security available depends in part on your device, operating system, and settings. No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Application or website changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Please review this page periodically to see the current policy.

10. Your Consent

By using the Application, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, do not use the Application.
